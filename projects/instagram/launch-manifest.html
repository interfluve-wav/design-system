4 · what I'd actually do next

Split the spine into clips. Right now the 30 s reel is one flat file — you can't retime or reorder anything. Splitting it into 8 individual MP4s (hook, twist, four features, sign-off, end card) makes it editable: you drag them onto a timeline in CapCut / Premiere / Resolve, keep the order, and swap in shot-library pieces between them.

Then rename everything by role. 01-hook-problem.mp4, 03-feature-bulk-edits.mp4, broll-logo-glow.mp4 — names that say what the clip is in the edit, so you never have to remember a version number again.

The one question I need from you: which editor? That decides the delivery format — cuts only (any editor), or a project file (Premiere / Resolve / Final Cut) with the clips already laid on a timeline at 120 BPM.
